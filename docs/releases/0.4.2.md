# WesomeCloud 0.4.2 preview

- Folders with dotted names such as `user@example.com` now open as folders in Finder. Recognized document packages keep their normal behavior.
- Upgrading rebuilds the Finder location once to clear cached folder classifications. Selected Spaces, sync caches, and queued uploads keep their existing storage IDs.

Upgrade note: downloaded and unsynced Finder files are preserved outside the replacement location. Their location is recorded in the diagnostic log. Check those preserved files for local edits.

Known limitation: automatic remote discovery can stop after the folder traversal limit, tracked in [#23](https://github.com/patriksimms/wesomecloud/issues/23).

Apple Silicon, macOS 15 or later. Open the DMG and drag WesomeCloud into Applications. Existing 0.1.3 or later installations can use Check for Updates.
